## Keeping Things Clean

### Summary

When working in WordPress, sometimes we may create something; a page, a CPT, a Custom Fields Group, and later find that we don’t need it anymore. If this is the case we should try to go back and remove unused elements to help keep things tidy.

## Text Formatting

### Summary

It’s good to keep the way that we name and label things consistent so that things are kept neatly and are easy to find and understand

### Titles

Any titles, be it for pages, CPTs, Field Groups, Content Templates or Views etc. should be capitalised, e.g. good:

* My Title
* My CPT Fields

Less good:

* My title
* My cpt fields
* my-cpt-fields

### Classes, IDs, Slugs and References

Any slugs or references should be lowercase with words separated by hyphens, e.g. good:

* my-custom-class
* my-content-template-reference
* here-is-my-slug

Less good:

* myCustomClass
* My-Content-Template_Reference
* here_is_my_slug
